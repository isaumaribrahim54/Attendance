About this system

Student Practical Attendance System

This system replaces the paper attendance sheet used during practical classes. A lecturer or administrator opens a session for a specific practical, with a defined opening and closing time. While the session is open, students mark themselves present directly from their own device. Once the session closes, the record is final: who attended, who didn't, and when each student checked in — all stored securely and available at a glance.

The goal is accuracy and accountability: no more misplaced sheets, no signing in for a classmate, and a clear attendance history for every student across every practical course.
